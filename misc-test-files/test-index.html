37033 LUCKY COWBOYS 4-H ROBOTICS

Robotics, teamwork, and a little grit.

Lucky Cowboys is FTC Team 37033, a robotics team founded through 4-H. We bring students together to explore engineering, programming, design, and problem-solving.

FIRST Tech Challenge gives us the opportunity to design and build a robot, compete with other teams, and learn from every challenge along the way.

Meet Our Team

Who We Are

We are students who enjoy building things, solving difficult problems, and learning something new every time we step into the lab.


Through 4-H and FIRST, we're not just building robots — we're building skills, friendships, and confidence.

More Than Robotics

Building robots is only part of what we do.

We believe STEM should be something everyone has the opportunity to experience. Through 4-H and FIRST, we work to share robotics, engineering, and technology with our community.

Our Community

We work to introduce younger students to robotics, help our community understand STEM, and encourage students to try building something of their own.
