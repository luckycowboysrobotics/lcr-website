01 / ABOUT

We build robots, but that's not all.

Lucky Cowboys is FTC Team 37033, a robotics team founded through the 4-H Club.

We design, build, program, and compete with robots while learning how to solve problems as a team.

FIRST gives us a place to put those skills to work and share what we learn with our community.
